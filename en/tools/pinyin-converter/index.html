Chinese to Pinyin

Convert Chinese characters to Pinyin with tone marks, tone numbers or no tones. Context-aware polyphone handling, initials-only output, character table, live conversion and local processing.

About this tool

Type Chinese text and get Hanyu Pinyin instantly. Readings are matched by phrase context, so polyphones such as 重庆 (chóng) and 银行 (háng) come out correctly.

Choose tone marks (hàn yǔ), tone numbers (han4 yu3) or plain pinyin, output initials only, keep or remove non-Chinese text, and inspect a character-by-character table.

Best use cases

  • Annotate documents, lyrics or subtitles with pinyin for learning and reading aloud.
  • Produce pinyin spellings of names and places for forms and international contexts.
  • Extract initials to build alphabetical indexes or sorting keys.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste Chinese text. Pinyin updates live.
  2. Switch tone style, output mode and non-Chinese handling; open the character table to verify polyphones.
  3. Copy the pinyin or download it as a TXT file.

Scope and limits

  • Up to 20,000 characters per conversion; the character table shows the first 1,000 Chinese characters.
  • Polyphones follow common phrase context; review special readings in names and places manually.

Data processing

Text is processed locally in your browser and never uploaded.

FAQ

How are polyphones resolved?

The pinyin-pro dictionary matches readings by phrase and context, e.g. 行长 reads háng while 行走 reads xíng. Isolated characters use the most common reading.

Is ü written as v?

No. Output uses standard ü (as in lǜ) by default.

Is my text uploaded?

No. After the dictionary loads with the page, everything runs locally in your browser.
