Simplified ⇄ Traditional

Convert between Simplified and Traditional Chinese with OpenCC dictionaries, phrase-level one-to-many handling, live conversion, copy and download. Everything runs locally in your browser.

About this tool

Type or paste Chinese text and convert between Simplified and Traditional instantly. Conversion uses OpenCC dictionaries locally in your browser, with phrase-level handling of one-to-many mappings such as 头发 → 頭髮.

Choose Simplified to Traditional (standard or HK), or Traditional to Simplified, and swap the result back for a quick round-trip check.

Best use cases

  • Convert Simplified documents, copy or subtitles to Traditional for readers in HK, MO and TW.
  • Turn Traditional web content or excerpts back into Simplified for reading and search.
  • Compare both variants side by side and verify proper nouns and variant characters.

How to use it

  1. Pick a direction and type or paste text. Conversion updates live.
  2. Review the result and use Swap to check the round trip when needed.
  3. Copy the result or download it as a TXT file.

Scope and limits

  • Up to 100,000 characters per conversion; split longer content into parts.
  • Dictionary-based conversion; review names, places and proper nouns manually.

Data processing

Text is processed locally in your browser and never uploaded.

FAQ

Is my text uploaded?

No. After the dictionaries load, conversion runs entirely in your browser.

Does it handle one-to-many characters correctly?

Yes. OpenCC matches phrases, so 头发 becomes 頭髮 and 后面 becomes 後面, which is more accurate than character-by-character mapping.

What is the difference between standard and HK Traditional?

The HK mode additionally applies Hong Kong and Macau character and phrase conventions on top of the standard Traditional conversion.
