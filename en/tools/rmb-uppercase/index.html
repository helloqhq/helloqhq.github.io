RMB Uppercase

Convert numeric amounts into Chinese RMB capital notation (壹贰叁肆伍陆柒捌玖拾佰仟万亿元角分整) live, with ¥ symbols, thousands separators and full-width digits support. Copy in one click, computed locally.

About this tool

Enter a numeric amount and get the standard Chinese capital form instantly, e.g. 1234.56 becomes 人民币壹仟贰佰叁拾肆元伍角陆分, ready for cheques, contracts and expense forms.

Accepts ¥/￥ symbols, thousands separators and full-width digits, and applies accounting rules for 零 and 整 automatically.

Best use cases

  • Fill in cheques, invoices, contracts and expense reports with correct capital amounts.
  • Cross-check capital amounts on financial documents against numeric values.
  • Prepare amount wording in bulk without handwriting errors.

How to use it

  1. Enter an amount, optionally with ¥ or thousands separators, up to two decimals.
  2. Review the live capital amount; toggle the 人民币 prefix as needed.
  3. Copy the capital amount into your document.

Scope and limits

  • Integer part up to 16 digits; at most two decimal places (jiao and fen).
  • Negative amounts are not supported; verify formal documents with your finance team.

Data processing

Amounts are computed locally in your browser and never uploaded.

FAQ

How are 零 and 整 handled?

Following accounting rules: 整 is appended when there are no decimals; 零 is inserted before fen when jiao is zero (100.05 → 壹佰元零伍分); consecutive zeros collapse into one.

How large can the amount be?

The integer part supports up to 16 digits, and decimals are limited to two places for jiao and fen.

Is my amount uploaded?

No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser, which suits sensitive financial data.
