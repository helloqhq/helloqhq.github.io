Full-width ⇄ Half-width

Convert between full-width and half-width characters live. Filter by letters, digits, punctuation and spaces using the standard Unicode mapping; Chinese text stays untouched. Copy, download and reverse-convert locally.

About this tool

Type or paste text and convert between full-width forms (ＡＢＣ１２３！) and half-width forms (ABC123!) instantly. The tool uses the standard Unicode mapping: ASCII !-~ maps to ！-～, and space maps to U+3000.

Toggle letters, digits, punctuation and spaces independently; unchecked categories pass through unchanged. Chinese characters and CJK punctuation without half-width counterparts are never modified.

Best use cases

  • Normalize full-width letters and digits produced by CJK input methods or legacy systems.
  • Convert half-width text to full-width to meet typography or data-entry conventions.
  • Clean mixed-width text so IDs, amounts and codes use a consistent character width.

How to use it

  1. Choose a direction: half-width to full-width, or full-width to half-width.
  2. Check the character categories to convert; results update live as you type.
  3. Review the output, swap to verify the round trip, then copy or download TXT.

Scope and limits

  • Up to 100,000 characters per conversion; split longer content into parts.
  • Only characters in the standard Unicode full-width/half-width mapping are converted.

Data processing

Text is processed locally in your browser and never uploaded.

FAQ

What is the difference between full-width and half-width?

Half-width characters occupy one standard character cell (ABC123); full-width characters occupy two (ＡＢＣ１２３). Full-width forms are common in CJK typography and legacy input methods.

Is Chinese punctuation converted?

Full-width forms such as ，！？：；（） have half-width counterparts and are converted. Characters like 。，、 have no half-width mapping and stay unchanged. Han characters are never modified.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser in real time.
