Barcode Generator

Generate Code128, EAN-13, UPC-A and Code39 barcodes locally. Validate check digits, adjust dimensions and text, and download PNG or SVG without uploading data.

About this tool

Generate barcodes from a value using Code128, EAN-13, UPC-A or Code39. Adjust bar width, height and text size, then download a PNG image or scalable SVG.

EAN-13 and UPC-A can complete a missing check digit or validate the check digit of a full number before generating an image.

Best use cases

  • Create labels for internal inventory, equipment or documents.
  • Render barcodes from existing product numbers.
  • Export vector barcodes for page layouts and printed materials.

How to use it

  1. Select a barcode type and enter a value or load the example.
  2. Adjust dimensions and text visibility, then check the preview and validation status.
  3. Download PNG or SVG and verify scanning after printing.

Scope and limits

  • Up to 128 characters. Code128 accepts printable ASCII; Code39 accepts uppercase letters, digits and selected symbols.
  • EAN-13 accepts 12 or 13 digits; UPC-A accepts 11 or 12 digits.
  • Keep the black-on-white contrast and quiet zones. This tool does not allocate or license GS1 numbers.

Data processing

Barcodes are generated in your browser. Input is not uploaded.
